Swap Monero without KYC: how the process works

  1. Choose your amount and destination

    Open the dashboard, select XMR as the asset to send, and choose the receiving coin. Paste its receiving address and check the network.

  2. Read the swap details

    Review the rate, expected payout, minimum amount and any deposit deadline. Keep enough XMR in your wallet for the sending fee.

  3. Send XMR and follow the order

    Send the requested amount to the order’s Monero address. The service releases the receiving asset after your Monero transaction confirms and the order is processed. Save the order ID.

How much does it cost to swap Monero?

The XMR sending fee
Your Monero wallet charges a network fee to broadcast the deposit.
The swap cost
A service fee or spread can be included in the exchange rate. Compare the final amount you receive.
The payout network fee
Sending BTC, USDT or ETH has its own network cost. Check whether it is included in the quote or deducted separately.

A floating rate moves with the market. A fixed-rate offer applies only under its stated amount and timing conditions.

Monero swap returned less than the quote?

Deposit still pending?

Check whether your wallet broadcast the transaction and whether it has confirmations. A pending deposit is not a reason to send it twice.

Quote expired?

Before sending, create a fresh order. If you already sent XMR, keep the transaction hash and contact the order’s support with your order ID.

Payout not visible?

Check the order for a payout transaction hash. Then check the receiving network, address and wallet sync status. Report a mismatch to support.

Monero swap questions, answered

Where can I swap Monero to BTC, USDT or ETH?

Open the dashboard to choose your Monero swap. Check that your receiving asset and network are available, then review the quote before sending XMR.

How long does it take to swap Monero?

The total time includes Monero deposit confirmations, order processing and confirmations on the receiving network. Follow the order status and its confirmation requirement for your transaction.

What is the minimum amount to swap Monero?

Use the minimum displayed in the current quote. It depends on the pair and swap costs. Check the deposit amount again after allowing for your wallet’s XMR sending fee.

Which network should I choose to swap Monero to USDT?

Choose a USDT network supported by both the order and your receiving wallet. The network on your wallet’s deposit screen must match the payout network exactly.

Do I need my seed phrase to swap Monero?

No. Use your receiving address to set up the order and authorize the XMR payment inside your own wallet. Never enter your seed phrase or private keys into a swap form or support chat.
